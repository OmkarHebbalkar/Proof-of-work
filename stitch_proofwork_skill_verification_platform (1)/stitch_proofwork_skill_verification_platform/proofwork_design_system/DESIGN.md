## Brand & Style
The design system establishes an institutional-grade, evidence-first visual environment engineered for authentic talent assessment and automated verification. It deliberately moves away from decorative recruitment marketing and generic resume boards, replacing subjective claims with objective telemetry, code execution logs, and rubric-driven scorecards.

The aesthetic philosophy balances **Technical Precision** with **Executive Authority**. It pairs deep slate structures and ultra-crisp white functional cards with electric indigo focus states and cryptographic emerald signals. Information hierarchy takes precedence: dense data grids, side-by-side evidence inspection panels, and benchmark comparisons must read with unambiguous clarity.

Key style pillars:
- **Calibrated Restraint:** High-contrast neutral structure where bright color is strictly semantic, indicating verified authenticity, flagged anomalies, or active system focus.
- **Structural Integrity:** Razor-thin slate boundaries, measured micro-dividers, and precise geometry that evoke developer consoles and financial ledgers.
- **Controlled Modernity:** Subdued tech-grid patterns and low-opacity surface reflections that signal modern infrastructure without compromising enterprise accessibility.

## Colors
The system operates on an asymmetric dual-surface structure: deep foundational anchor regions (navigation rails, terminal docks, contextual inspectors) set against clean, high-efficiency light workspaces for intensive document and rubric review.

- **Primary Base (`#0F172A` / `#0A1128`):** High-density ink used for structural navigation shells, metric headers, and high-impact baseline typography.
- **Action Indigo (`#4F46E5` / hover `#4338CA` / light tint `#EEF2FF`):** Reserved for primary interactive controls, active evaluation steps, selected benchmark parameters, and forward progress indicators.
- **Verification Emerald (`#10B981` / border `#059669` / surface `#ECFDF5`):** Reserved strictly for positive cryptographic proofs, satisfied rubric requirements, and tamper-proof verification badges.
- **Review Amber (`#F59E0B` / surface `#FFFBEB`):** Denotes candidate discrepancies, manual human-in-the-loop review queues, or edge-case score deductions.
- **Neutral Boundaries & Grounding (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8FAFC`, `#E2E8F0`, `#CBD5E1`):** Crisp pure-white card planes sit elevated on `#F8FAFC` canvas backdrops, bound by `#E2E8F0` structural rules.

## Typography
The system employs a dual-sans typographic hierarchy reinforced by a monospaced coordinate tier:

- **Display & Section Anchors (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Handles high-level positioning, module headers, and candidate verification scores. Its contemporary geometry provides authority and forward momentum without decorative artifice.
- **Analytical & System Context (Inter):** Drives analytical tables, rubric criteria, feedback breakdowns, and candidate submission narratives. Its uniform glyph metrics ensure clean tabular alignment during multi-column data reviews.
- **Evidence Telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied to candidate commit hashes, timestamp verifications, execution outputs, and raw metric telemetry.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a strict 8pt structural rhythm across desktop, tablet, and mobile environments.

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views operate on a 12-column adaptive fluid grid within a 1440px maximum display bounds. Split-screen verification consoles divide into a 4-column rubric/metric sidecar (380px pinned minimum) and an 8-column fluid artifact viewing canvas.
- **Adaptation Rules:**
  - **Desktop (>=1200px):** Simultaneous display of candidate artifact (code/design/report), AI-evaluated rubric benchmarks, and verified execution logs.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** Inspector panels collapse into tabbed side-sheets; 8-column layout with `gutter-sm`.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column stacked stream. Sticky bottom action sheets replace docked verification panels; layout margins collapse to `margin-mobile`.
- **Rhythm Principle:** Internal card padding is strictly uniform using `space-md` (16px) or `space-lg` (24px). Micro-alignments between labels, evidence tags, and metric values lock to `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px).

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through strict structural borders and tonal layering rather than heavy ambient blur.

- **Ground Level (Canvas):** Tone `#F8FAFC` serves as the neutral staging floor.
- **Level 1 (Evidence & Score Cards):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a 1px solid `#E2E8F0` hairline border, accompanied by a precise downward drop: `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0px 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Inspections):** Elevated workspace cards transition to `0px 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0px 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)` with border tinting to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Command Modals & Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces framed with subtle perimeter glows (`0 0 0 1px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.1), 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1)`).
- **Subtle Tech Accents:** Background gridlines (1px wide on 32px centers at 4% opacity of `#0F172A`) may sit behind technical proof displays, providing structural rhythm without visual clutter.

## Shapes
The system relies on compact, disciplined radii that convey architectural stability:
- **Base Components (`rounded` / 0.5rem / 8px):** Standard controls, text input boxes, score breakdown strips, and dropdown selectors.
- **Card Containers (`rounded-lg` / 0.75rem / 12px):** Verification cards, rubric evaluation boards, and artifact viewer frames.
- **Modals & Overlays (`rounded-xl` / 1rem / 16px):** System dialogues and deep-dive evidence modals.
- **Verification Pills (`rounded-full`):** Cryptographic signatures, verification status badges, and candidate tier pills use fully rounded endpoints to separate metadata chips from structural cards.

## Components

### Verification Badges & Status Chips
- **Verified Badge:** Emerald container (`#ECFDF5`), 1px solid emerald stroke (`#A7F3D0`), emerald text (`#065F46`), and a 6px solid `#10B981` status dot. Uppercase tracking (`label-sm`).
- **Needs Review Chip:** Amber container (`#FFFBEB`), 1px solid border (`#FDE68A`), text (`#92400E`), accompanied by a mini warning glyph.
- **Skill Credential Tag:** Slate container (`#F1F5F9`), slate text (`#334155`), with an integrated monospaced percentile rating (e.g., `TOP 3%`).

### Interactive Buttons
- **Primary Action (Issue Verification, Confirm Rubric):** Solid `#4F46E5` background, white text (`Inter`, 600 weight), 8px border radius. Hover: `#4338CA`. Active: slight downward translation with a 2px `#C7D2FE` focus ring.
- **Secondary Action (Inspect Raw Data):** `#FFFFFF` surface, 1px solid border (`#CBD5E1`), text (`#0F172A`). Hover: `#F8FAFC`, border color `#94A3B8`.
- **Destructive/Revoke Action:** Pale crimson background (`#FEF2F2`), border (`#FCA5A5`), text (`#991B1B`).

### Rubric Scorecards & Cards
- Constructed with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), pure `#FFFFFF` background, and 12px rounding.
- Header section includes rubric name, baseline target, and live benchmark comparison meter (a 6px multi-segment bar displaying candidate tier vs. global cohort).
- Footer houses immutable hash strings rendered in `code-sm` typography alongside human reviewer sign-offs.

### Form Inputs & Telemetry Fields
- Height of 40px, `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px solid border (`#CBD5E1`), placeholder text in `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state activates an electric indigo outline (`0 0 0 2px #EEF2FF`, border color `#4F46E5`).
- Read-only technical fields (e.g., repository commit anchors, SHA-256 proofs) employ a `#F8FAFC` background with a subtle dashed `#CBD5E1` border and terminal-styled copy shortcuts.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Checkboxes: 18x18px squares with 4px border radius. In checked state, solid `#4F46E5` background with a crisp white check vector.
- Radios: Circular 18px controls with a centered 8px solid disc upon selection.

### Split Verification Inspector (Specialized Component)
- A dual-panel interface: Left panel displays candidate submissions (code diffs, structured answers, linked designs) within a contained `#0F172A` terminal or light PDF frame; Right panel stacks verifiable scoring rubrics with inline slider controls, confidence percentages, and AI analysis callouts.
